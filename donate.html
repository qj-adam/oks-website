Support our work

Email us about donating

Claiming a tax credit. OKS is a registered charitable entity and an approved donee organisation, so if you’re a New Zealand taxpayer you can claim a tax credit of 33.33% on donations of $5 or more — $33.33 back on a $100 gift. You’ll need a receipt, so just email info@oks.nz and we’ll send you one. Claims are made through your myIR account with Inland Revenue at the end of the tax year, and you can claim for the past four years if you’ve missed any.

Bequests & in-memoriam gifts

OKS has been very fortunate to receive several bequests over the years that have allowed us to do more for our community than we ever could alone.

If you’re considering leaving a bequest to OKS, or making a donation in memory of someone special, we would be honoured to talk with you and your family about how your gift can be used.

Please email info@oks.nz, or phone our treasurer Karen Macleod on 021 280 0552, and we can arrange a quiet conversation.

Fundraisers & partners

We are deeply grateful to the local businesses, clubs and community groups who choose OKS as their charity of the year — like the Otago Classic Motoring Club and the Otago Early Falcon and Fairlane Club, who named us as their charity for the Auto Spectacular and raised $4,000 for our work.

If your club or business is looking for a local charity partner with a clear, practical impact, we’d love to talk.
