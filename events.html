Events & news

Lunches, World Kidney Day, the AGM, and other gatherings of the Otago renal community. Everyone is welcome — members, whānau, and friends.

Coming up

—Winter '26

Winter member lunch

Our winter get-together — venue and date confirmed in the Winter 2026 newsletter. As always, OKS contributes $10 toward each member’s meal. Bring a partner, friend or whānau member.

—Spring '26

Spring member lunch

Our third lunch of the year — usually at a relaxed venue in Dunedin or Central Otago. Watch the newsletter for the date.

—Dec '26

Christmas lunch

Our annual Christmas lunch — always a festive afternoon with great company. Past locations have included the Mosgiel Tavern and the Moeraki Tavern.

12Mar

World Kidney Day stand

Join us in central Dunedin (Wall Street Mall) for our annual World Kidney Day stand, in partnership with Kidney Health New Zealand. Free blood-pressure checks and kidney function tests, plus information for everyone.

—AGM

Annual General Meeting

Held each autumn at Dunedin Hospital. All members welcome. Receive the annual report and audited financials, elect the committee, and have your say on the year ahead.

Want exact dates? Subscribe to our quarterly newsletter — the easiest way to keep in the loop. Email info@oks.nz and we’ll add you to the list.

Why we lunch

“It was lovely to meet other people in the same situation, and I didn’t feel so lonely or isolated.” That’s a comment we hear at almost every lunch.

If you’ve never been to one — give it a try. They’re relaxed, they’re welcoming, and you don’t need to know anyone to come along. We organise four a year, in different parts of Otago, so most members can get to one without a long drive.

OKS contributes $10 toward each member’s meal. We typically hold them in pubs or cafes that can offer a private room or a quiet corner.

News & recent activity

Welcoming our new patron

2025 / 2026

We’re honoured to welcome Katherine Rich as our patron. Some of you will remember her father, the late Jock Ellison — a former OKS President who fought tirelessly for organ donation reform. Katherine is keen to meet members and to champion our cause with ministers.

$5,000 to the Christchurch Dialysis Treatment Van

2025

OKS donated $5,000 toward the new dialysis treatment van — a vital service used by a number of our members. In return, Christchurch Kidney Society sent us black bags we now use for our welcome gift packs.

A new ice machine for the Unit

2025

The previous ice machine at the Dunedin Dialysis Unit had stopped working — so OKS purchased a replacement. Small things make a real difference for patients spending hours in treatment.

Thank you, Stew MacLeod

2025

It was with great sadness that we lost one of our biggest committee supporters, Stew MacLeod. He championed our causes and quietly distributed our brochures across hospital departments, doctors’ surgeries and community spaces. We miss him very much.
