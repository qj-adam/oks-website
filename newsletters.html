Our quarterly newsletter

Four times a year we put together a newsletter for our members — news from the Dunedin Dialysis Unit, kidney health updates from across New Zealand, recipes, member stories, and a heads-up on what’s coming next at OKS. Every issue is free to download.

Want the next issue in your inbox?
