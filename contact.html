Get in touch

Whether you’re a patient, a whānau member, a clinician, a fundraiser, or just curious about what we do — we’d love to hear from you.

Our main contact is by email — we’re a small volunteer team, but we’ll always come back to you within a few days.

What can OKS help with?

Common reasons people get in touch:

  • You’re newly diagnosed or new to dialysis and would like a welcome pack or someone to chat with.
  • You’d like to be added to our mailing list and receive the quarterly newsletter.
  • You’re thinking about coming to a member lunch and want to know when the next one is.
  • Your whānau would like to make a donation in memory of someone.
  • Your business or club would like to choose OKS as a charity partner.
  • You’re a clinician who would like brochures or supplies for your waiting room.
  • You’d like to volunteer or stand for the committee.

A note about privacy

We only use your personal information to communicate with you, send you newsletters and information, and keep you in the loop about OKS news. We don’t share your details with anyone outside the Society without your permission.
